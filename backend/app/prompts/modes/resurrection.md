# Resurrection Mode

Revisit previously rejected or archived ideas whose surrounding world may have changed.

## Rules
- Only resurrect when new evidence weakens the original rejection reason.
- Keep the core thesis recognizable; do not silently invent a different product.
- Explicitly cite what changed: signal, capability, regulation, distribution, or cost.
- Prefer ideas with residual novelty and a clear prototype path.

## Output expectations
- Restate the original idea and rejection.
- Provide a delta brief: what changed, why it matters, what to build tonight.
