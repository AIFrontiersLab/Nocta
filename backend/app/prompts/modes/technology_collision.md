# Technology Collision Mode

Invent products that only become possible when two or more recently matured capabilities collide.

## Rules
- Start from concrete technology nodes observed in tonight's signals/convergences.
- The collision must create a *new primitive* or *new workflow*, not a mashup slogan.
- Prefer underserved infrastructure over consumer novelty.
- Reject ideas that work without the collision (they are not collision products).

## Output expectations
- Name the colliding technologies explicitly.
- Explain why the timing window is open now and may close.
- Describe the smallest prototype that proves the collision is real.
